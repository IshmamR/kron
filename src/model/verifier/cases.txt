# Fact–line pairs for measuring the verifier. Each case: its label, the
# facts the line cites, then the line. Fails are small edits of valid lines,
# so the fabrication is the only difference.
#
# Labels:
#   paraphrase, number_rewording, combined, job_wording, kept_qualifier,
#   same_scope: should pass
#   invented_figure, invented_year, unit_swap, wrong_referent,
#   scope_inflation, invented_term, unrelated, borrowed_figure, cross_entry,
#   job_echo, dropped_qualifier: should fail
#
# borrowed_figure cases cite two facts and take a figure from the one that
# isn't about the line.
#
# A fact may name the entry it's from, a job or project, as `fact E1: …`.
# combined and cross_entry cases cite facts from one entry and from two.
#
# A case may give the job the résumé is for, as `job: …`. job_echo lines
# take a word from it that their facts don't say; job_wording lines use
# its words only where their facts say the same.
#
# dropped_qualifier lines drop or add a word that changes whether or how
# far something happened: proposed, planned, started, tried, without, not.
# scope_inflation lines claim a bigger part: assisted becomes built, built
# becomes led, worked in a team becomes led one. same_scope lines change
# the verb to one no higher.
